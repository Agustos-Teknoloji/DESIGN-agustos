How to choose

Look at body weight

Squint at each option. Which body feels closest to "comfortable book reading" — neither aggressive nor anemic? That's the right body weight for you.

Look at bold contrast

Find "specification discipline" in each cell. Which option lets your eye land on it without effort? If it doesn't pop, the contrast is wrong.

Look at the link

The link is bold + colored underline. Whatever bold you choose affects link weight too. Make sure links read as confident, not aggressive.

Look at long reading

Read the full Turkish paragraph in each option. After two sentences, do you want to keep reading or rest? The right body is the one your eye doesn't tire on.

Watch for fragility

Options A and C use weight 380 — non-standard, lightest. Look at small letters (ı, ş, ğ in Turkish). Do they hold their character or feel weak?

Watch for warmth

Option D's high SOFT may feel softer/warmer or it may feel "too rounded." It's the most editorial-feeling option but also the least conventional.
