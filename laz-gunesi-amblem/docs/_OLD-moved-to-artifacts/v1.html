Logotype specimen — choosing the wordmark for Ağustos

Five candidates rendered next to the Laz Güneşi at three sizes (hero · meeting · header lockup). All in brand red #cf142a, no subtitle, no underline. Decide by eye.

The ğ in "Ağustos" is the load-bearing glyph. Look at how its breve sits — too light and it disappears at small sizes; too heavy and it dominates. Look at the rhythm of the descender on g, the spacing around A, and how the wordmark balances the symbol's visual mass.
