Logotype specimen v2 — slim, lowercase, four brands

Five candidates, each rendering all four publisher brands in their own brand color: ağustos, pataraz, pld türkiye, photometric batch. Hero + small (header lockup) sizes. All wordmarks lowercase. Decide by eye.

Test glyphs: ğ in ağustos, ü in türkiye, plus how the font handles a short word (pataraz) vs. a long compound (photometric batch). The long names are stress tests for letter-spacing and rhythm.
