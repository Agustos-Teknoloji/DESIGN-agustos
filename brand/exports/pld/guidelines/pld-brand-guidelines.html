1Introduction

PLD Türkiye is part of the Ağustos family: Ağustos Teknoloji, Pataraz, PLD Türkiye, IESDesk, SpecQuick, Emre Güneş, Banu Uçak and Selim Güneş. Every brand shares one symbol, the Laz Güneşi, an 18-blade sun. Ağustos alone uses red. The other brands use black or white, and their wordmark tells them apart.

Our design direction is İskandivvian: Scandinavian restraint filtered through Mediterranean warmth. In practice, that means white paper, few colours, clear type and plain language. Every element must be useful. Nothing is there for decoration.

This guide covers the basics: the logo, colour, type and the right file for each job. Use it when you make anything that carries the PLD Türkiye name.

3The symbol

The Laz Güneşi is an 18-blade sun. Every brand in the family uses this one symbol. Use the supplied artwork only. Never redraw, simplify or rearrange the blades.

Colour of the symbol

In the PLD Türkiye logo, the symbol is off-black, like the wordmark. On a dark background, it is white. As a favicon or app icon, the symbol stands alone, off-black on a white square tile.

4The logo

The logo is the symbol with the wordmark "pld türkiye", set in Inter Tight at weight 650, always lowercase. The two parts are one unit. Do not move them apart or use the wordmark without the symbol.

Three versions

Pick the version by the background. There is no fourth version.

Positive
Default. Light backgrounds, about 90% of uses.
Negative
On the identity colour, dark colours and photographs.
Mono
One ink only: stamps, engraving, single-colour print.

8Colour in use

On screens, colour follows the state of each element. Every pair below clears the WCAG 2.2 AA floor: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for borders, logos and the focus ring. The same pairs hold in the light and the dark theme.

  • Red is identity and signal, never action. Buttons are black in every brand. Red marks the Ağustos logo, the 2px link rule, the current menu item, keyboard focus and one highlighter stroke.
  • Hover. On light paper a link turns red. On dark paper red text is too faint (3.35:1), so the text dims and the red rule stays. The Ağustos logo turns off-black (white on dark); every other logo turns red.
  • Pressed and disabled. A pressed button moves 1px down. A disabled control turns gray and does not react.
  • Dark theme. Every page starts light. The user may switch to dark; the device never chooses. The footer and the closing band turn dark with the page. Print is always light.

9Typography

Three typefaces, all free and open source. Headings are thin and large; weight, not colour, sets the order. Write in sentence case. Sizes follow one scale: the 16.5px body times 1.272 per step, so every second step is the golden ratio.
