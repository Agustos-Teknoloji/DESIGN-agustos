Colour

Six colours. White paper. Off-black ink and black buttons. Red for identity and signal, never action. Dark theme reuses the same six, flipped. Identity ink names the brand. Those jobs must not collapse into one token.

Substrate

About 70% of every surface is white. Pale red is the one closing band on a page. Dark is the user's choice on any page, through the theme switch. It uses the same six colours, flipped.

Identity and signal

Ağustos identity is red. Every other house brand identity is off-black. Shared signal red is always #cf142a: the 2px link and menu rule, keyboard focus, and one highlighter stroke per page. On the web the logo answers hover: the Ağustos logo turns black, every other logo turns red. Buttons are black. Never fill a button, a statistic, or text with red.

Signal / Ağustos--signal #cf142a

State

Success, warning, danger, and info sit on notices and badges. Dark theme swaps these for higher-contrast variants. Use kit classes. Do not invent hex values.

Dark theme

Locked. No new hexes. Paper, surface, callout, ink, and ink-soft reuse the six colours. Buttons invert with the ink: the primary is white on off-black. Red never fills a button.

Paper#15130f
Off-black becomes the substrate.

Surface#404040
Cards and panels on dark paper.

Callout#ebebeb
The closing band on dark.

Ink#ffffff
Headlines and primary text.

Ink soft#8a8378
Secondary text on dark.

Red#cf142a
Unchanged. Rules, focus, and the logo only.

Do not

  • Follow the device setting for the theme, or start a page dark.
  • Fill a button, a statistic, or a background with red, or set text in red.
  • Use pale red as the page paper or as an inset rounded card.
  • Mix a pale red band and a gray band on the same page.
  • Replace red links with terracotta, olive, or beige.
  • Add gradients or a second accent colour. The only shadow sits under a floating menu.
  • Invent a seventh palette hex. A new value needs a deleted one. Dark theme reuses the six.
  • Retype a hex in a consumer project. Use var(--name).
