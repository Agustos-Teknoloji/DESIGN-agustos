Colour

White paper. Off-black ink. Shared red for action. Identity ink names the brand. Those jobs must not collapse into one token.

Substrate

About 70% of every surface is white. Cream is a full-bleed callout band only. Dark is opt-in, not the default editorial look.

Identity and signal

Ağustos identity is red. Every other house brand identity is off-black. Shared signal red is always #cf142a for links, focus, markers, and small emphasis. Never recolor a non-Ağustos logo red. Never fill a button with signal red.

Signal / Ağustos--signal #cf142a
Action. Also Ağustos identity ink.

House identity--brand #15130f
Pataraz, PLD, IESDesk, SpecQuick.

State

Success, warning, danger, and info sit on notices and badges. Dark theme swaps these for higher-contrast variants. Use kit classes. Do not invent hex values.

Success Warning Danger Info Signal

Do not

  • Use cream as the page paper or as an inset rounded card.
  • Mix a cream band and a gray band on the same page.
  • Replace red links with terracotta, olive, or beige.
  • Add shadows, gradients, or a second accent colour.
  • Retype a hex in a consumer project. Use var(--name).
