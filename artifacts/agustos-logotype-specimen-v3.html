Logotype specimen v3 — slim, structural, magazine

Five new candidates extending the direction you liked in Bricolage Grotesque Light + Bodoni Moda Light: slim + structural confidence + magazine-modern feel. Three sans (Space Grotesk, Manrope, Familjen Grotesk) and two serifs (Playfair Display, Italiana). All free OFL, all self-hostable.
