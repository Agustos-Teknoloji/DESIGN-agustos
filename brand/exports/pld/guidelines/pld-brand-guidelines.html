The logo

The mark is the Laz Güneşi symbol locked up with the wordmark. Three expressions cover every surface. Use the positive form first — it works on white and dark backgrounds alike.

Positive — primary
Negative — on brand
Mono — single colour

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the symbol height. Never place type or edges inside it. Minimum size: 24px tall on screen, 8mm in print, so the wordmark stays legible.

Do & don't

  • Do set the wordmark in Inter Tight Semibold, lowercase, in the registered identity ink.
  • Don't recolour the symbol, stretch the lockup, or add a tagline beside it.
  • Do use shared red for interaction and emphasis; don't recolour a non-Ağustos logo red.

Typography

Two families, one skeleton. Inter Tight for display and the wordmark; Inter for body. JetBrains Mono for code and data.
