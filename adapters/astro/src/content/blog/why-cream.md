The default web background is white. That is now the paper of this system.

We used to set cream as the page. It read as editorial, and it kept red, black, and white quiet. Version 5 keeps that warmth, but it moves cream off the page and onto the band.

## What the paper is for

White `#ffffff` is the reading surface. Marketing, catalog, and spec pages ship on it. Type-only pages are complete. A gray well may mark a reserved photo slot. Do not wait for photography to ship the page.

## What cream is for

Cream `#fdf5f5` is a full-bleed callout. Hairline rules sit on the top and bottom. Use it for:

1. A closing CTA band that repeats the page primary destination.
2. A short callout that needs to step off the paper.

Never use cream as inset rounded card fill. Never use it as the page paper. Logo red at 5% into white is the mix; do not invent a seventh hex.

## What this gives you back

Three things:

1. A calm reading surface that does not compete with type.
2. One cream band that can close the page without putting a primary button in every section.
3. The same six colours on dark product UI, flipped. Marketing stays light.

The `--paper` and `--cream` variables still swap in one line. Same tokens, same rules. Cream is a band, not a substrate.
