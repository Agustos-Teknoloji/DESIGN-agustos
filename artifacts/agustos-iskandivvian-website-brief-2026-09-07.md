# İskandivvian website brief

Date: 2026-09-07
Status: Proposed direction for discussion and implementation planning.

## Definition

İskandivvian is the working concept name in this brief.
Treat it as a defined project label, not a reference to an established design movement.

Scandinavian restraint filtered through Mediterranean warmth.

Create a minimal, functional, and elegant website that feels calm, welcoming, and human.
Combine clear structure and low visual noise with warm neutrals, comfortable spacing, and approachable language.
Texture is optional. Flat colors, typography, and layout can fully express this style.

## Intended experience

Visitors can understand the offer, find useful information, and act without effort.
The site feels considered and trustworthy, with enough warmth to invite exploration.
Elegance comes from proportion, clarity, and care.

Keywords: calm, clear, warm, functional, elegant, approachable, grounded, quietly confident.

## Translate the concept into website decisions

| Principle | Website application |
|---|---|
| Restraint | Give each section a clear purpose. Remove competing emphasis and unnecessary containers. |
| Function | Make navigation, links, forms, and next actions easy to understand. |
| Warmth | Use cream or warm white, comfortable spacing, and readable type. |
| Human presence | Use direct copy, useful explanations, and authentic stories or images when available. |
| Natural light | Choose photography with believable daylight and clear subjects. Preserve accurate product appearance. |
| Material honesty | Show real products and materials when relevant. Do not simulate physical materials in interface controls. |
| Softness | Use balanced proportions and modest corners. Preserve precise alignment and clear hierarchy. |
| Calm | Limit simultaneous accents, dense framing, decorative movement, and repeated calls to action. |

## Color and identity

Use existing cream, white, and dark themes as the starting point.
Test warm neutral refinements in representative pages before selecting new token values.
Keep dark text strong enough for comfortable reading.
Preserve white for practical uses and dark mode for user needs.

Keep Ağustos red `#cf142a` for its identity and shared interaction signals.
Keep other house-brand identities black or white, according to their registered expressions.
Do not replace red links or focus indicators with terracotta, olive, or beige.
Earth tones can appear naturally in photographs. They are not a required interface palette.

## Typography and composition

Retain Inter Tight for display, Inter for body text, and JetBrains Mono for technical content.
Preserve lowercase Inter Tight 650 wordmarks and the existing Laz Güneşi symbol.
Test display weight, line spacing, and text measure before considering another font family.
Make hierarchy clear without making every heading oversized or heavy.

Use one consistent alignment frame with generous but purposeful spacing.
Allow editorial layouts to vary image size and column balance.
Stack content in a logical order on mobile.
Keep forms and technical tables compact enough for their tasks.
Do not use excessive whitespace as a substitute for useful content.

## Imagery and texture

Use imagery when it explains the product, place, people, or work.
Prefer real installations, believable daylight, and accurate material detail.
Avoid generic lifestyle images that add atmosphere without useful context.
Do not apply warm filters that distort product finishes or lighting performance.

Texture is neither required nor a measure of success.
Do not add grain overlays, paper backgrounds, plaster effects, or simulated linen to make a page comply.
If source photography contains texture, let it remain within the image.
Keep text, forms, tables, and controls on plain surfaces.

## Interface and language

Use simple buttons with clear labels and modest corners.
Use borders and surface differences where they help users recognize groups or controls.
Use shadows only when they clarify layering.
Keep hover and focus feedback clear, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Write with direct verbs and familiar words.
Explain practical benefits and next steps.
Make errors specific and helpful.
Avoid inflated luxury claims, vague slogans, and forced friendliness.

## Boundaries

Avoid ornamental Mediterranean motifs, oversized pill controls, decorative motion, and unnecessary interface colors.
Do not soften text or control contrast to create atmosphere.
Do not interpret this brief as permission to change logos, brand inks, or product facts.
Load the generated UI kit and fonts. Consumer projects must use existing token variables.
Propose shared source changes in this repository when the kit cannot express a repeated need.

## Review questions

1. Can visitors understand the page and its next action quickly?
2. Does the page feel warm and approachable without texture or imagery?
3. Does spacing clarify relationships while keeping useful content within reach?
4. Do images explain something relevant and preserve factual accuracy?
5. Do controls, text, and focus states remain clear in every supported theme?
6. Does the page remain recognizably part of the Ağustos brand family?

## Reusable LLM instruction

Apply Scandinavian restraint with Mediterranean warmth to this website.
Use clear hierarchy, simple layouts, purposeful spacing, warm neutrals, and direct, approachable language.
Create an elegant, calm, functional experience with low visual noise.
Texture is optional; the design must feel complete on flat surfaces without imagery.
Use authentic, naturally lit photographs only when they support the content.
Preserve the existing brand identity, font families, red interaction signals, accessibility rules, and generated token contract.
Judge the result by clarity, warmth, and ease of use.
